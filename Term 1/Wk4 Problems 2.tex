\begin{center}
\huge{\textbf{Sequences}}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Determine $$\lim\limits_{n\to\infty} (2^n+3^n)^\frac{1}{n}.$$
    
    \item \begin{enumerate}[(i)]
    \item Show that if $(a_n)$ and $(b_n)$ are convergent sequences with $a_n\to \alpha$, $b_n\to\beta$ then
    \[
    \max\{a_n,b_n\} \to \max\{\alpha,\beta\},\quad\min\{a_n,b_n\} \to \min\{\alpha,\beta\}.
    \]
    \emph{(hint: think of a clever way to write $\max\{x,y\}$ you could apply algebra of limits to)}
    \item Give an example of divergent sequences $(a_n)$ and $(b_n)$ for which $\max\{a_n,b_n\}$ and $\min\{a_n,b_n\}$ both converge. Show, however, that if $(a_n)$, $\max\{a_n,b_n\}$ and $\min\{a_n,b_n\}$ all converge, then $(b_n)$ is also convergent.
    \end{enumerate}
    
    \item Let $(a_n)$ and $(b_n)$ be sequences of positive numbers such that, for $n\geq 1$,
    \[
    a_{n+1} = \frac{1}{2}(a_n+b_n),\quad b_{n+1} = \sqrt{a_nb_n}
    \]
    \begin{enumerate}[(i)]
        \item Show that for all $n\geq 2$, $(a_n)$ is monotone decreasing and $(b_n)$ is monotone increasing.
        \item Deduce that $(a_n)$ and $(b_n)$ have the same limit.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
\huge{\textbf{Bonus (Olympiad Style)}}
\end{center}

A $3\times k$ board has every cell coloured black or white. Such a board is called:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{sandwich-free} if there are no three adjacent cells in any row or column which form a ``sandwich'': a black cell surrounded by white cells or a white cell surrounded by black cells, i.e. \black\white\black ~or \white\black\white. 
    \item \emph{three-free} if there are no three adjacent cells in any row or column that are the same colour, i.e. \black\black\black ~or \white\white\white.
    \item \emph{four-balanced} if every four adjacent cells in any row have the same number of black and white cells (two of each).
\end{itemize}

Let $S$ be the number of sandwich-free $3\times 2026$ boards, $F$ be the number of three-free $3\times 2026$ boards, and $B$ be the number of four-balanced $3\times 2026$ boards.
Determine $S+B-F$.
